## What the reviewer attacked and could not disprove

- The hook's behaviour: 1px height sweeps in seven configurations (320px at 100/150/200% text among them) give exactly two flips, a 13px hysteresis band and zero clipped, short-body or nested-scroller steps; jitter across both thresholds gives at most one flip; rotation and text-size changes while open, safe-area insets, forced-colors (2px borders), opening already pinch-zoomed or with the keyboard up, 25 open/close cycles (no listener leak, no ResizeObserver loop error) and StrictMode all behave; Tab/Shift+Tab, Escape, Back-dismiss, inert background, scroll lock and focus return work in the page layout.
- Accessibility: no colour or focus change in the diff, `--tap` and the viewport meta unchanged, pinch zoom still works; the echo is `aria-hidden` with a test.
- Scope: clean; no licensed content, no secrets in the evidence folder.

## Not verified by this reviewer (and by whom, or not at all)

Real Mobile Safari (the iOS Simulator runs were read from logs only; the author's re-run is in the evidence README), physical devices, Dynamic Type, Android font scale, Android Chrome with `interactive-widget=resizes-content` on hardware, VoiceOver/TalkBack/NVDA, Windows High Contrast beyond CSS emulation, the emulator suite, the two-device smoke, `npm run build` and `npm run check` (all run by the author afterwards and recorded in `CLAUDE_HANDOFF.md`), staging, and the dice-dependent injury picker.
