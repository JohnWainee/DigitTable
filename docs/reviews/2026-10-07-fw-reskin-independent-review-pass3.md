are in `155a62b`.

## What the reviewer attacked and could not disprove

- **The reveal fix:** no reveal on mount; exactly one reveal per flip (a sweep plus jitter around the threshold gave two reveals for two flips, the 12px hysteresis held); page to pinned puts the
  focused checkbox inside the body; a focused heading is harmless, a focused Cancel is revealed; Escape returns focus to the trigger and releases inert and the scroll lock, and the Back-dismiss
  scenario passes. The unit test is real: the text-only, both-paths and no-reveal mutants, `block: center` and the other reveal mutants are killed, as are all nine of the pass-2 hook mutants.
- **The action row:** equal halves at every integer width 280-700px at 16, 20, 24 and 32px roots and at 4,852 fractional widths (380-560px, DPR 1 and 3); no needless stacking, no mid-word
  break, targets 48px or more; RTL, forced colors and reduced motion are fine; the focus ring against the drop shadow is as before (12px gap against a 5px shadow plus a 6px ring); the old
  grid mutant and the no-`min-content` mutant are caught (3 and 16 failures).
- **Labels:** 20px from 286px up at default text, never smaller than the old value, never below 1rem. **The Reveal row:** clean at 280-1280px x 100-200%, emulated and with a real 32px font.
- **The harness:** a variant that reveals text fields only after a flip fails `sheet-flip-keeps-focused-control-in-view`. **Scope:** the patch is identical to the worktree against `97cc9c7`; the
  stylesheet compiles to exactly the served CSS; presentation, tests and harness only; no licensed text; no secrets found by a pattern scan of the evidence folder; prettier, eslint and tsc
  clean; the sheet and contract tests passed.
